# Shattered Realms AI
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version: 0.1

---

# Vision

Shattered Realms AI aims to become the first AI tabletop RPG platform that truly feels alive.

Instead of simply generating text adventures, the game creates persistent worlds where every decision permanently changes the story, NPCs remember your actions, battles require real tactical thinking, and AI brings adventures to life through dynamic visuals.

Our goal is simple:

> Become the first AI game that truly feels like playing with a world-class Dungeon Master.

---

# Mission Statement

Build the world's best AI-powered tabletop RPG where players can create and play unlimited adventures in any universe with meaningful choices, tactical combat, and living worlds.

---

# Core Pillars

## 1. Living Worlds

The world never forgets.

- NPCs remember conversations.
- Kingdoms react to player choices.
- Cities evolve over time.
- Characters can permanently die.
- Quests can permanently fail.
- Nothing magically resets.

The player exists inside the world.

The world does not exist for the player.

---

## 2. Any Universe

Players aren't limited to fantasy.

They can create campaigns inspired by almost any original setting or genre.

Examples:

- Fantasy
- Space Adventures
- Pirate Worlds
- Zombie Survival
- Cyberpunk
- Original Superhero Worlds
- Detective Mysteries

The AI builds the campaign around the player's prompt.

---

## 3. Hardcore Rules

Inspired by our Hardcore Ruleset.

Examples:

- Permanent consequences
- Public dice rolls
- Exact HP
- Exact inventory
- Initiative
- Skill checks
- Saving throws
- Adaptive enemy AI
- No plot armor

Every victory should feel earned.

---

## 4. Tactical Combat

Combat should feel like commanding a squad instead of reading text.

Features include:

- Initiative order
- Grid-based movement
- Speed stat
- Terrain costs
- High ground
- Cover
- Opportunity attacks
- Line of sight
- Companion commands
- Tactical positioning
- Environmental interactions

Players should have to think.

---

## 5. AI Visuals

The AI doesn't only tell stories.

It shows them.

Examples:

- Battlefields
- NPC portraits
- Monsters
- Cities
- Castles
- Forests
- Bosses
- Important story moments

Combat updates visually every round.

---

# Combat Philosophy

The battlefield itself is part of the puzzle.

Examples:

- Push enemies off cliffs.
- Burn forests.
- Freeze rivers.
- Collapse bridges.
- Hide in smoke.
- Destroy cover.
- Knock over statues.

Players should constantly look for creative solutions.

---

# Battlefield System

Combat uses a tactical grid.

Each creature has a movement speed.

Example:

Knight: 4 tiles

Mage: 5 tiles

Archer: 6 tiles

Rogue: 7 tiles

Wolf: 8 tiles

Movement is affected by terrain.

Examples:

Grass

Forest

Mud

Snow

Water

Ladders

Cliffs

Players use natural language.

Example:

"Run behind the wagon."

The AI determines the correct path across the battlefield.

---

# Companion Commands

Instead of controlling every character individually, players may issue squad commands.

Examples:

Focus Fire

Protect Ally

Hold Position

Advance

Defensive Formation

Fall Back

Each companion also has their own AI personality.

---

# Enemy AI

Enemies fight intelligently.

Each enemy has a combat role.

Examples:

Tank

Archer

Assassin

Mage

Commander

Healer

Enemies coordinate instead of attacking randomly.

Bosses adapt to player strategies during combat.

---

# Battle Replay

Major battles automatically generate cinematic highlight reels.

Players can replay:

Boss fights

Epic victories

Close defeats

Memorable moments

These clips are designed to be easily shared.
