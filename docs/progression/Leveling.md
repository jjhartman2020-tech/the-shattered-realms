# 1. Leveling Philosophy

## Design Goals

The leveling system is designed to reward every type of player.

Players should become stronger through exploration, intelligent decision making, completing quests, defeating powerful enemies, and discovering the world—not simply by repeatedly farming enemies.

Progression should always feel rewarding, fair, and meaningful.

Design Goals:

- Reward every playstyle.
- Encourage exploration.
- Avoid unnecessary grinding.
- Make every level meaningful.
- Allow complete build freedom.
- Reward difficult accomplishments.

# 2. Experience (XP Orbs)

The game uses **XP Orbs** instead of traditional experience points.

XP Orbs are permanently earned and cannot be lost.

Whenever enough XP Orbs have been collected, the player immediately levels up.

Every mission clearly displays its XP Orb reward before being accepted.

Example:

Goblin Camp

Reward:
• 3 XP Orbs

Difficulty:
Easy

Additional XP Orbs may be earned through:

- Optional Objectives
- Hidden Discoveries
- Bosses
- Exceptional Accomplishments
- Secret Areas
- Bonus Objectives

---

# 3. Level Rewards

Every level grants:

- +5 Maximum Health
- +5 Attribute Points
- +1 Ability Point

These rewards are granted every level from Level 2 through Level 97.

---

# 4. Attribute Points

Each level grants five Attribute Points.

Players may freely distribute these points between any attributes.

Attributes:

- Health
- Mana
- Strength
- Dexterity
- Constitution
- Intelligence
- Wisdom
- Charisma
- Speed

Every attribute has a natural maximum value of **60**.

Equipment, buffs, temporary effects, consumables, and abilities may increase attributes beyond 60.

However, a player's base attributes can never permanently exceed 60.

---

# 5. Ability Points

Each level grants one Ability Point.

Ability Points are used to permanently unlock abilities.

Players may save Ability Points to unlock stronger abilities later.

Abilities never gain experience and never level up.

Progression comes from unlocking new abilities rather than upgrading existing ones.

---

# 6. Maximum Level

The maximum player level is **97**.

This value is determined mathematically by the progression system.

Players begin with:

- 60 Attribute Points

There are:

- 9 Attributes

Each attribute has a natural maximum of:

- 60

Maximum possible Attribute Points:

540

Starting Attribute Points:

60

Remaining Points Required:

480

480 ÷ 5 Attribute Points per level = 96 Level-Ups

Therefore:

Level 1 + 96 Level-Ups = **Level 97**

Level 97 is therefore the natural maximum level.

# 7. Experience Sources

XP Orbs may be earned through many different activities.

Examples include:

- Completing Quests
- Optional Objectives
- Defeating Bosses
- Exploration
- Discovering Hidden Locations
- Solving Puzzles
- Story Progression
- Companion Quests
- Faction Quests
- World Events
- Exceptional Achievements

Combat is only one path to progression.

Players should never feel forced into combat simply to level up.

# 9. Quest Reward Guidelines

Quest rewards should generally follow these ranges.

| Difficulty | Typical XP Orb Reward |
|------------|----------------------:|
| Trivial | 1–3 |
| Easy | 3–8 |
| Medium | 8–20 |
| Hard | 20–50 |
| Expert | 50–100 |
| Legendary | 100–250 |
| World Boss / Final Story | 250–500 |

These values are guidelines rather than strict rules.

The AI may increase or decrease rewards depending on:

- Quest Length
- Difficulty
- Risk
- Optional Objectives
- Story Importance
- World Impact
- Player Decisions

---

# 10. Progression Balance

## Design Goals

- Every level should feel meaningful.
- Every Attribute Point should matter.
- Every Ability Point should feel valuable.
- Exploration should always be rewarding.
- Multiple playstyles should progress equally well.
- Grinding should rarely be necessary.

A player who completes the game's content naturally should be capable of reaching the maximum level without repetitive farming.
