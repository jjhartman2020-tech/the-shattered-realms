# 1. Leveling Philosophy

The leveling system rewards exploration, intelligent decisions, quests, powerful enemies, discoveries, story progress, and other meaningful accomplishments rather than repetitive farming.

---

# 2. Experience (XP Orbs)

The game uses **XP Orbs** instead of traditional experience points.

XP Orbs are permanently earned and cannot be lost. Whenever enough XP Orbs have been collected, the player immediately levels up.

---

# 3. Level Rewards

Every level after Level 1 grants:

- +5 Maximum Health
- +3 Attribute Points
- +1 Ability Point

---

# 4. Attribute Points

Players may freely distribute Attribute Points among the nine attributes:

- Health
- Mana
- Strength
- Dexterity
- Constitution
- Intelligence
- Wisdom
- Charisma
- Speed

Every attribute has a natural base maximum of **30**.

Equipment, buffs, temporary effects, consumables, abilities, and special world effects may exceed 30 when their own rules permit it.

For d20 attribute rolls, every full 3 points grants +1 to the roll. Example: 17 Strength gives +5 to Strength rolls.

---

# 5. Ability Points

Each level grants one Ability Point.

Ability Points permanently unlock abilities. Players may save them for stronger abilities. Abilities do not gain experience or level up unless another formally documented system explicitly changes this rule.

---

# 6. Maximum Level

The maximum player level is **71** under the base progression system.

Players begin with 60 Attribute Points.

There are 9 attributes, each with a natural cap of 30:

`9 x 30 = 270 maximum base Attribute Points`

Starting Attribute Points:

`60`

Remaining points needed to reach the natural cap in every attribute:

`270 - 60 = 210`

Each level-up grants 3 Attribute Points:

`210 / 3 = 70 level-ups`

Therefore:

`Level 1 + 70 level-ups = Level 71`

Level 71 is the natural maximum level for the base progression system.

---

# 7. Experience Sources

XP Orbs may come from quests, optional objectives, bosses, exploration, hidden locations, puzzles, story progression, companion quests, faction quests, world events, and exceptional achievements.

Combat is only one path to progression.

---

# 8. XP Orb Requirements

The XP Orb curve will be rebalanced for the new Level 71 maximum before the full leveling loop is finalized.

Until that balance pass is complete, the previous Level 97 XP table is deprecated and must not be treated as authoritative.

Design targets remain:

- Early levels arrive quickly.
- Mid-game progression remains steady.
- Late-game progression is challenging without excessive grinding.
- Reaching Level 71 should be a major accomplishment.

---

# 9. Quest Reward Guidelines

| Difficulty | Typical XP Orb Reward |
|------------|----------------------:|
| Trivial | 1–3 |
| Easy | 3–8 |
| Medium | 8–20 |
| Hard | 20–50 |
| Expert | 50–100 |
| Legendary | 100–250 |
| World Boss / Final Story | 250–500 |

These values are guidelines and may be rebalanced alongside the Level 71 XP curve.

---

# 10. Progression Balance

- Every level should feel meaningful.
- Every Attribute Point should matter.
- Every Ability Point should feel valuable.
- Exploration should remain rewarding.
- Multiple playstyles should progress fairly.
- Grinding should rarely be necessary.

---

# 11. Future Systems

Possible future additions include Prestige Levels, New Game+, Seasonal Characters, Hardcore Progression, World Scaling, Level Sync, and Mentor systems.
