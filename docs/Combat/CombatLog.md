# Combat Log Philosophy

The Combat Log records, communicates, and summarizes everything that occurs during combat.

Its purpose is to ensure that players understand the current state of the encounter while maintaining a clear, engaging, and consistent narrative.

The Combat Log should balance mechanical clarity with immersive storytelling.

Players should never struggle to determine what has happened or what their current situation is.

---

# Combat Narration

The AI Game Master should narrate combat in a way that is both informative and engaging.

Narration should clearly describe:

- Participant Actions
- Results
- Environmental Changes
- Status Effects
- Important Battlefield Events
- Objective Progress

Descriptions should remain consistent with the campaign's tone and setting.

---

## Clarity

Combat narration should prioritize clarity over unnecessary detail.

Players should immediately understand:

- Who acted.
- What action occurred.
- Who was affected.
- The outcome of the action.
- Any important consequences.

Clear communication reduces confusion and helps maintain the pace of combat.

---

## Narrative Style

Campaign creators may choose different narration styles appropriate to their setting.

Examples include:

- Cinematic
- Minimal
- Tactical
- Dramatic
- Realistic
- Humorous
- Horror
- Narrative-Focused

The chosen style should remain consistent throughout the campaign.

---

# Action Resolution

Every resolved action should be communicated in a logical order.

The AI Game Master should generally communicate:

1. The action being attempted.
2. Whether the action succeeds.
3. The immediate results.
4. Any additional effects.
5. Changes to the battlefield.

Following a consistent structure helps players quickly understand combat events.

---

## Significant Events

Some moments deserve additional emphasis.

Examples include:

- Critical Outcomes
- Boss Phase Changes
- Participant Defeat
- Objective Completion
- Environmental Changes
- Major Ability Usage
- Narrative Turning Points

Significant events should be highlighted without interrupting the overall flow of combat.

---

## Maintaining Pace

Combat narration should maintain an appropriate pace.

Routine actions may be described briefly, while important moments should receive additional attention.

The AI Game Master should avoid unnecessary repetition while ensuring that important information is never omitted

---

# Combat History

The Combat Log should maintain an accurate record of important events throughout an encounter.

Players should be able to understand how the current battlefield state was reached without needing to remember every individual action.

The Combat Log serves as both a narrative record and a tactical reference.

---

## Recorded Events

The Combat Log should record significant combat events, including:

- Participant Actions
- Damage Dealt
- Healing Received
- Status Effects Applied or Removed
- Resource Usage
- Defeated Participants
- Objective Progress
- Environmental Changes
- Boss Phase Transitions

Campaigns may choose to record additional information when appropriate.

---

## Combat Summaries

During lengthy encounters, the AI Game Master may provide summaries of recent events.

Summaries should highlight information such as:

- Current Objectives
- Active Threats
- Remaining Allies
- Remaining Enemies
- Major Battlefield Changes
- Important Status Effects
- Recent Significant Events

Summaries should improve player awareness without replacing detailed narration.

---

# Information Management

The AI Game Master should communicate information according to what participants could reasonably perceive.

Not every combat detail should be immediately known to every participant.

Information should be presented fairly, consistently, and in accordance with the campaign's rules.

---

## Public Information

Examples of information that is typically available include:

- Visible Actions
- Obvious Environmental Changes
- Known Objectives
- Observable Injuries
- Defeated Participants
- Clearly Visible Status Effects

Public information should be communicated clearly to all relevant participants.

---

## Hidden Information

Some information may remain intentionally concealed.

Examples include:

- Hidden Enemies
- Secret Objectives
- Invisible Participants
- Concealed Traps
- Undiscovered Weaknesses
- Unrevealed Boss Mechanics

Hidden information should only be revealed when participants have a reasonable opportunity to discover it.

---

## Consistency

Information should remain internally consistent throughout the encounter.

The AI Game Master should avoid:

- Revealing hidden information accidentally.
- Contradicting previous descriptions.
- Omitting significant events.
- Providing knowledge participants could not reasonably possess.

Maintaining consistency helps preserve immersion and player trust.

---

# Visibility

The amount of information presented should match the current situation.

Routine combat actions generally require concise descriptions.

Important moments deserve greater detail.

The AI Game Master should adjust the level of detail based on the significance of the event rather than applying the same level of narration to every action.

---

## Tactical Visibility

Players should always receive enough information to make informed decisions.

The Combat Log should clearly communicate:

- Current Battlefield Conditions
- Active Objectives
- Known Threats
- Available Opportunities
- Significant Environmental Features
- Immediate Consequences of Recent Actions

Players should never lose because essential information was withheld unfairly.

---

## Narrative Visibility

Not every mystery should be explained immediately.

Campaigns may intentionally conceal information to create:

- Suspense
- Mystery
- Discovery
- Dramatic Reveals
- Story Progression

Hidden information should enrich the narrative without making player decisions feel arbitrary or uninformed.

---

# Balance Philosophy

The Combat Log exists to communicate combat clearly while enhancing the player's immersion in the encounter.

Its purpose is not simply to record actions, but to help players understand the evolving battlefield, make informed decisions, and experience memorable moments.

A well-designed Combat Log should remain informative without becoming overwhelming.

---

## Readability

Combat information should be presented in a logical and easy-to-follow manner.

Players should never struggle to determine:

- Whose turn it is.
- What action occurred.
- How the battlefield changed.
- Which objectives remain.
- Which threats require immediate attention.

Clear communication improves both pacing and tactical decision-making.

---

## Consistency

The AI Game Master should present combat information using a consistent structure throughout the campaign.

Consistency includes:

- Similar narration style.
- Consistent terminology.
- Predictable ordering of information.
- Reliable summaries.
- Accurate historical records.

Consistency allows players to focus on strategy rather than interpreting changing presentation styles.

---

## Player Agency

The Combat Log should support player decision-making by presenting relevant information without dictating choices.

Players should be able to:

- Evaluate risks.
- Plan future actions.
- Track important developments.
- Learn enemy behavior.
- Respond to changing objectives.

The Combat Log should provide the information necessary for meaningful decisions while preserving uncertainty where appropriate.

---

## Narrative Integration

Combat narration should reinforce the campaign's story.

Descriptions should reflect:

- Character Personalities
- Enemy Behavior
- Environmental Changes
- Emotional Stakes
- Story Progression
- World Events

Mechanics and narrative should complement one another rather than feeling disconnected.

---

# Future Systems

The Combat Log framework is designed to support future expansion without changing its core principles.

Possible future additions include:

- Replayable Combat Histories
- Searchable Combat Records
- Timeline Visualizations
- AI-Generated Battle Summaries
- Spectator Mode
- Campaign Analytics
- Performance Statistics
- Dynamic Narration Profiles
- Campaign-Specific Logging Modules

Future systems should continue to prioritize clarity, consistency, player understanding, and immersive storytelling while remaining adaptable to different genres and campaign styles.
