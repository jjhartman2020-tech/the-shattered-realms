# 1. Combat Philosophy

Combat in **The Shattered Realms** is designed to be tactical, meaningful, and story-driven.

Every encounter should feel unique, rewarding intelligent decision-making over repetitive strategies or overwhelming statistics.

Victory should come from planning, positioning, teamwork, resource management, and creative use of abilities rather than simply having the highest numbers.

Combat is only one possible solution to a problem.

Whenever appropriate, players should also have opportunities to negotiate, retreat, surrender, avoid conflict, or achieve objectives without eliminating every opponent.

---

# 2. Design Goals

The combat system is built around the following goals:

- Tactical Decision Making
- Player Freedom
- Meaningful Choices
- Fair Challenge
- AI-Driven Encounters
- Dynamic Battlefields
- Minimal Random Frustration
- Fast but Thoughtful Gameplay
- Genre Flexibility

Every combat mechanic should support one or more of these goals.

---

# 3. Core Principles

## Every Turn Matters

Players should have meaningful decisions every turn.

No turn should feel wasted because there are always multiple tactical options available.

---

## Information Creates Better Decisions

Players should understand enough about the battlefield to make informed choices.

Important information should be communicated clearly while still allowing uncertainty and surprises when appropriate.

---

## The World Responds Naturally

Enemies should behave according to their intelligence, training, morale, personality, and objectives.

Combat should feel like fighting believable opponents rather than scripted enemies.

---

## Creativity Should Be Rewarded

The environment, abilities, equipment, status effects, and AI systems should interact naturally.

Players should be encouraged to solve problems creatively rather than relying on a single optimal strategy.

---

## Fair Does Not Mean Easy

Combat may be challenging, but players should understand why they succeeded or failed.

Unexpected difficulty should come from believable circumstances rather than hidden mechanics or unfair advantages.

---

# 4. Combat Overview

Combat follows a consistent structure.

Every encounter generally progresses through the following stages:

1. Combat Begins
2. Awareness
3. Initiative
4. Turn Order
5. Player & AI Actions
6. Round Resolution
7. Victory, Retreat, Surrender, or Defeat
8. Rewards & Consequences

Each stage is explained in greater detail throughout the combat documentation.

# Relationship With Other Systems

Combat works together with many other core systems but does not redefine them.

Examples include:

- **Abilities** determine what actions characters can perform.
- **Equipment** determines the statistics of weapons, armor, and gear.
- **Status Effects** define buffs, debuffs, and ongoing effects.
- **Leveling** determines progression and character growth.
- **Inventory** manages usable items during combat.
- **Reputation** and **Crime** determine the consequences of combat outside the battlefield.

Each system acts as the single source of truth for its own mechanics.

Combat is responsible only for resolving encounters using those systems.

---

# 6. Balance Philosophy

Every combat mechanic should contribute toward creating encounters that are:

- Fair
- Tactical
- Challenging
- Rewarding
- Replayable

The combat system should reward preparation, creativity, teamwork, and intelligent decision-making while avoiding repetitive or unfair gameplay.

No single strategy, class, ability, or piece of equipment should consistently outperform every alternative.

Combat should remain engaging throughout an entire campaign regardless of setting, level, or party composition.

---

# Future Expansion

The combat framework is designed to grow without requiring major redesign.

Future additions may include:

- Naval Combat
- Vehicle Combat
- Mounted Combat
- Siege Warfare
- Large-Scale Battles
- Space Combat
- Underwater Combat
- Competitive PvP
- Cooperative Raid Encounters

Future systems should build upon the existing combat framework while preserving its core philosophy of tactical depth, player freedom, and meaningful decision-making.
