# 1. Inventory Philosophy

## Design Goals

- Inventory management should be simple but meaningful.
- Every item should have value.
- Players should think carefully about what they carry.
- Inventory decisions should create interesting gameplay rather than unnecessary frustration.
- Inventory should support the Hardcore Rules by making death meaningful.

---

# 2. Inventory Types

Items are divided into categories:

- Weapons
- Armor
- Shields
- Accessories
- Consumables
- Crafting Materials
- Quest Items
- Keys
- Valuables
- Miscellaneous

These categories help organize and filter the player's inventory.

---

# 3. Inventory Capacity

Players have a limited inventory.

Inventory size may be expanded through:

- Bags
- Backpacks
- Magical Storage
- Equipment
- Skills
- Special Rewards

Inventory limits should encourage planning without becoming tedious.

---

# 4. Equipment Inventory

Equipment includes:

- Weapons
- Armor
- Shields
- Accessories

Equipment may be equipped or stored inside the inventory.

Only equipped items provide their bonuses.

---

# 5. Consumables

Consumables include:

- Health Potions
- Mana Potions
- Buff Potions
- Food
- Scrolls
- Throwables

Consumables are destroyed after use unless otherwise specified.

---

# 6. Quest Items

Quest Items:

- Cannot be accidentally sold.
- Cannot be destroyed unless required by the story.
- May unlock quests, locations, dialogue, or world events.

---

# 7. Crafting Materials

Crafting materials are used to create:

- Weapons
- Armor
- Potions
- Enchantments
- Quest Items

Some materials are extremely rare and should encourage exploration.

# 8. Currency

Players may:

- Drop

- Sort
- Filter
- Favorite
- Inspect

Every item displays:

- Effects

# 12. Hardcore Rules

## Death

When a character dies, every item they were carrying is dropped at the location of death.

This includes:

- Equipped Weapons
- Equipped Armor
- Shields
- Accessories
- Backpack Contents
- Consumables
- Crafting Materials
- Gold

Dropped items remain in the world and may be:

- Recovered by the player.
- Collected by companions.
- Looted by enemies.
- Stolen by NPCs.
- Destroyed by environmental hazards when applicable.

If the items are not recovered, they are permanently lost.

Players should carefully consider the risk of bringing valuable equipment into dangerous situations.

Death is intended to have meaningful consequences.

---

# 13. Balance Philosophy

## Design Goals

- No item should feel useless.
- Inventory management should reward preparation.
- Rare items should feel exciting to obtain.
- Losing equipment should create memorable stories rather than frustration.
- Every inventory decision should matter.
