# Crafting Philosophy

## Purpose

Crafting exists to reward exploration, experimentation, and mastery.

It is not simply a menu for converting materials into equipment.

Players should feel like true craftsmen who learn through experience, discover forgotten techniques, gather rare resources, and create items that become part of their story.

Every crafted item should feel earned.

---

## Core Principles

### Crafting Is a Profession

Crafting is a long-term progression system.

Players improve through practice, discovery, experimentation, and experience rather than simply unlocking recipes.

Master craftsmen should feel genuinely accomplished.

---

### Discovery Through Exploration

Powerful recipes should rarely be purchased from ordinary merchants.

Players discover new knowledge by:

- Exploring ruins
- Reading ancient journals
- Completing quests
- Learning from master craftsmen
- Studying lost civilizations
- Finding forgotten workshops
- Defeating legendary enemies

Exploration should naturally expand crafting possibilities.

---

### Experimentation

Players are encouraged to experiment.

Known recipes make crafting easier and more reliable.

However, experienced craftsmen may successfully create items without already knowing the recipe.

Experimentation rewards creativity while still respecting progression.

---

### Materials Matter

Materials define an item's identity.

Different materials may affect:

- Damage
- Armor
- Weight
- Durability
- Appearance
- Value
- Special effects
- Elemental properties

Two swords made from different materials should feel like entirely different weapons.

---

### Skill Matters

The skill of the crafter affects the final result.

Higher skill may improve:

- Crafting quality
- Bonus attributes
- Material efficiency
- Success rate
- Crafting speed

Skill represents knowledge rather than luck.

---

### Failure Creates Learning

Failure should never feel unfair.

Poor craftsmanship may produce:

- Lower-quality items
- Reduced durability
- Increased material consumption
- Fewer bonuses

Failure should teach rather than punish.

---

### Legendary Crafting

Legendary equipment requires legendary effort.

Creating the world's greatest items may require:

- Ancient blueprints
- Extremely rare materials
- Powerful crafting stations
- Assistance from master craftsmen
- Multiple crafting disciplines
- Significant quests

Legendary equipment should feel like an achievement rather than another recipe.

---

### Living World Integration

Crafting interacts with the rest of the world.

It influences:

- Equipment
- Economy
- Merchants
- Exploration
- Quests
- Reputation
- Companions
- Housing
- Factions

Crafting should feel like a natural part of the world's economy rather than an isolated menu.

---

## Design Philosophy

Crafting should reward knowledge instead of repetition.

The goal is not simply to manufacture equipment.

The goal is to create stories of legendary weapons, forgotten recipes, master craftsmen, and discoveries that players remember long after they are forged.

# Recipes & Discovery

Recipes teach players reliable methods of creating items.

Learning a recipe permanently adds it to the player's Crafting Journal.

Recipes may be discovered through:

- Exploration
- Ancient books
- NPC teachers
- Quests
- Dungeons
- Ruins
- Merchants
- Crafting experimentation

Recipes improve reliability but are not always required.

Experienced craftsmen may successfully create items through experimentation alone.

---

# Crafting Skill

Each crafting discipline has its own independent skill progression.

Improving one discipline does not automatically improve others.

Higher crafting skill may provide:

- Better quality
- Greater efficiency
- Faster crafting
- Reduced material waste
- Access to advanced recipes
- Improved experimentation success

Crafting skill improves naturally through crafting, experimentation, quests, training, and discovering new techniques.

---

# Crafting Quality

Every crafted item has a quality rating.

Higher quality generally results in stronger equipment or more effective consumables.

Quality tiers include:

- Poor
- Common
- Fine
- Superior
- Masterwork
- Legendary

Higher quality may improve:

- Damage
- Armor
- Healing
- Duration
- Durability
- Bonus attributes
- Value

Quality depends on:

- Crafting skill
- Materials
- Crafting station
- Recipe complexity
- Special circumstances

Legendary-quality items should remain exceptionally rare.

---

# Experimentation

Players are encouraged to discover new crafting methods.

Experimentation allows players to combine materials without already knowing a recipe.

Successful experimentation may result in:

- Discovering new recipes
- Creating unique equipment
- Learning improved techniques
- Unlocking hidden combinations

Failed experimentation should not feel overly punishing.

Possible outcomes include:

- Lower quality items
- Wasted time
- Partial material loss
- Unusable prototypes

Experimentation rewards creativity while ensuring that recipes remain valuable for consistency and reliability.

# Crafting Journal

The Crafting Journal records everything the player has learned throughout their journey.

The journal automatically updates whenever the player:

- Learns a new recipe
- Discovers a new material
- Unlocks a new crafting discipline
- Successfully experiments
- Creates a legendary item
- Learns a new crafting technique

The journal is intended to organize crafting knowledge rather than act as a checklist.

Unknown recipes, materials, and techniques remain hidden until discovered.

# Balance Philosophy

Crafting exists to complement exploration, combat, quests, and the economy.

It should never become mandatory for players who prefer other playstyles.

Players who invest time into crafting should gain meaningful rewards without making found or purchased equipment obsolete.

Crafting should reward:

- Exploration
- Knowledge
- Experimentation
- Long-term investment
- Resource management

The strongest equipment should often require a combination of:

- Rare materials
- Difficult quests
- Skilled craftsmanship
- World exploration

No single crafting discipline should become universally superior.

Every profession should have valuable and unique contributions throughout the game.

Crafting progression should feel rewarding from the beginning of the game through the endgame.
