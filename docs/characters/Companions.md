# Companion Philosophy

## Purpose

Companions are not followers or equipment.

They are fully realized characters who exist independently of the player and continue living whether or not they ever join the player's party.

The goal is to make every companion feel like the protagonist of their own story.

---

## Core Principles

### Independent Lives

Companions continue living whether the player recruits them or not.

They may:

- Become heroes
- Become villains
- Join factions
- Rise to power
- Die
- Travel the world
- Start families
- Pursue their own ambitions

The world never waits for the player.

---

### Every Companion Is a Main Character

Every recruitable companion should have:

- Their own personality
- Their own goals
- Their own fears
- Their own dreams
- Their own morality
- Their own memories
- Their own relationships
- Their own reputation
- Their own combat style
- Their own equipment
- Their own questline
- Their own schedule

No companion should exist solely to support the player.

---

### Opinions and Reactions

Companions naturally react to the player's decisions.

They may:

- Agree
- Disagree
- Argue
- Defend another companion
- Suggest alternatives
- Refuse requests
- Praise good decisions
- Condemn bad ones

Their reactions are based on their personality, memories, values, and trust—not a simple approval meter.

---

### Relationships

Companions build relationships with one another over time.

Possible relationships include:

- Friends
- Rivals
- Mentors
- Family-like bonds
- Mutual respect
- Distrust

These relationships continue evolving throughout the game.

---

### Trust and Loyalty

Trust is earned through actions over time.

Highly trusted companions may:

- Reveal secrets
- Defend the player
- Risk their lives
- Unlock unique dialogue
- Follow difficult decisions

Low trust may result in:

- Arguments
- Refusing requests
- Hiding information
- Temporary separation
- Permanent departure

---

### Personal Goals

Every companion has personal ambitions unrelated to the player.

Examples include:

- Revenge
- Redemption
- Protecting family
- Becoming stronger
- Discovering lost knowledge
- Leading a kingdom
- Restoring honor

Players may choose to help, ignore, or oppose these goals.

---

### Permanent Consequences

Companions can:

- Leave the party
- Die permanently
- Change sides
- Become enemies
- Become rulers
- Sacrifice themselves

Every major decision should have lasting consequences.

---

### Living World Integration

Companions participate in the world's systems just like every other important NPC.

This includes:

- Dialogue
- Reputation
- Schedules
- Combat
- Quests
- Equipment
- Progression
- Relationships
- Memory

Companions obey the same world rules as everyone else.

---

## Design Philosophy

The goal is not to create party members.

The goal is to create believable people.

Players should remember the companions they traveled with, argued with, laughed with, trusted, lost, and changed—not because the game told them to, but because those relationships developed naturally through the world itself.
