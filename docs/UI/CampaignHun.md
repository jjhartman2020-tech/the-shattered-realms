# Campaign Hub

## Purpose

The Campaign Hub provides players with a centralized overview of their entire adventure.

Rather than presenting detailed information owned by individual interfaces, the Campaign Hub summarizes progress and provides intuitive navigation throughout the application.

It acts as the player's command center between gameplay sessions.

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- Campaign overview
- Progress summaries
- Navigation
- Dashboard organization
- AI summaries
- Activity overview
- Accessibility
- Customization

This document does not define:

- Quest mechanics
- World mechanics
- Character systems
- Inventory mechanics
- Party mechanics
- Dialogue systems

Those mechanics remain documented within their respective systems.

---

# Hub Philosophy

The Campaign Hub should answer one primary question:

"How is my adventure going?"

Rather than overwhelming players with details, the hub should summarize meaningful information while making deeper exploration effortless.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Campaign Hub is more than a dashboard.

It is the player's command center.

Every major system within the game eventually connects here, allowing players to understand their adventure without requiring them to manually gather information from multiple interfaces.

Rather than replacing specialized interfaces, the Campaign Hub provides context, summarizes meaningful information, and guides players toward their next destination.

Every feature should answer one of three questions:

"Where am I in my adventure?"

"What has changed since I last played?"

"Where should I go next?"

If a feature cannot help answer one of those questions, it should be reconsidered before becoming part of the dashboard.

The Campaign Hub should make returning to the game effortless, helping players reconnect with their story regardless of whether they last played yesterday or a year ago.

---

# Summary

The Campaign Hub provides a centralized, accessible, and AI-assisted overview of the player's entire adventure.

It presents campaign progress, story summaries, world status, companion activity, quest highlights, exploration, collections, statistics, recent events, and intelligent recommendations through a unified dashboard while leaving gameplay mechanics to their respective systems.

By emphasizing context, continuity, navigation, and personalization, the Campaign Hub transforms disconnected systems into a single, coherent view of the player's unique journey through The Shattered Realms.
