# Party Interface

## Purpose

The Party Interface provides a centralized location for viewing, organizing, and interacting with the player's active companions.

Rather than defining companion mechanics, this interface presents party information in a structured and accessible format.

The Party Interface helps players understand the current state of their team while providing intuitive navigation to companion-related information.

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- Party presentation
- Companion organization
- Interface navigation
- Party summaries
- Formation visualization
- Companion interaction
- Accessibility
- Customization

This document does not define:

- Companion mechanics
- Relationship systems
- Equipment mechanics
- AI behavior logic
- Combat mechanics

Those systems remain documented within their respective folders.

---

# Party Interface Philosophy

A party should feel like a living group of individuals rather than a list of units.

Every companion should communicate personality, current condition, and role through presentation alone.

Players should immediately understand:

Who is present.

Who needs attention.

Who is leading.

Who is unavailable.

The interface should encourage emotional attachment rather than simple resource management.

# Design Philosophy

A party is more than a collection of statistics.

It is a group of individuals whose shared experiences shape the player's journey.

The Party Interface should help players remember the people who fought beside them, the relationships they built, the hardships they overcame, and the moments that defined their adventure.

Every section should answer one of three questions:

"Who is with me?"

"How are they doing?"

"What story have we shared together?"

If a feature does not help answer one of those questions, it should be reconsidered before becoming part of the interface.

The Party Interface should feel less like managing units and more like revisiting the history of trusted companions.

---

# Summary

The Party Interface provides a centralized, immersive view of the player's companions.

It presents party composition, relationships, assignments, formations, shared history, and AI-generated summaries through a consistent, accessible interface while leaving gameplay mechanics to their respective systems.

By emphasizing personality, narrative continuity, and intuitive navigation, the Party Interface transforms party management into an extension of the player's story, ensuring that companions feel like living members of the world rather than interchangeable game pieces.
