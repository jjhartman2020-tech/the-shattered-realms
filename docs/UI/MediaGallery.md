# Media Gallery

## Purpose

The Media Gallery provides players with a centralized interface for viewing, organizing, and revisiting visual media collected throughout their adventure.

Rather than acting as simple image storage, the Media Gallery serves as a visual archive of the player's unique campaign.

The interface celebrates memorable moments while providing intuitive navigation through every unlocked piece of media.

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- Media presentation
- Gallery organization
- Search
- Filtering
- Collections
- Navigation
- Accessibility
- Customization

This document does not define:

- Unlock mechanics
- Achievement mechanics
- Quest mechanics
- AI image generation
- Screenshot capture implementation

Those systems remain documented elsewhere.

# Gallery Philosophy

The Media Gallery should answer one primary question:

"What has my adventure looked like?"

The interface should encourage players to revisit important moments while discovering artwork they may have forgotten.

# Design Philosophy

The Media Gallery is more than an image viewer.

It is the museum of the player's adventure.

Every portrait, every landscape, every cinematic, every memory, and every piece of artwork represents a moment that mattered within the campaign.

Rather than storing images as isolated collectibles, the gallery preserves the visual history of the player's decisions, discoveries, companions, victories, and losses.

Every feature should answer one of three questions:

"What have I seen?"

"Why was this moment important?"

"How does this connect to my adventure?"

If a feature cannot help answer one of those questions, it should be reconsidered before becoming part of the gallery.

The Media Gallery should allow players to relive their adventure visually, transforming hundreds of hours of gameplay into a permanent collection of meaningful memories.

---

# Summary

The Media Gallery provides a comprehensive, accessible, and visually rich archive of the player's adventure.

It presents artwork, character portraits, landscapes, cinematics, memories, screenshots, collections, AI-generated illustrations, and campaign albums through a unified interface while leaving gameplay mechanics to their respective systems.

By emphasizing visual storytelling, historical preservation, organization, and discovery, the Media Gallery transforms unlocked artwork into a living museum that celebrates each player's unique journey through The Shattered Realms.
